Garnet Fields

The Garnet Fields are a stretch of mineral-rich land where red soil and scattered geysers shape the landscape. Crops here grow faster than anywhere else in Tugarth, making the region quietly valuable.

The Garnet Fields are a wide expanse of red-stained earth where small garnet fragments lie scattered across the ground. The soil is rich and warm, shaped over time by mineral deposits rising from below.

Steam geysers break the surface at irregular intervals, releasing bursts of heat that drift across the fields. Crops grown near these vents develop faster than expected, maturing in a fraction of the usual time.

While not as strategically vital as other regions, the fields provide a steady supply of food and basic materials, supporting nearby Tug settlements with quiet reliability.
