Riverrun

Riverrun used to be the main trading hub of Tugdor, bringing in goods from Togmar and functioning as a harbour and workshop for all aquatic vehichles. After the diggings in crimson peak, the earth has become incresingly hotter and the once thriving river Riverrun used to run of has begun to dry up; trapping Tugdor's boats and keeping the tugs earthbound.
